        Shinobi AR

        Puts the Sharingan, Mangekyō, Rinnegan and Byakugan on your own eyes and reads your hand seals to cast jutsu. It needs your webcam. Video stays on this device.

        Guide

        These are simplified so the hand tracker can tell them apart. Keep both hands in view, close together, palms to the camera, and hold until the ring fills. Prefer the real interlocked seals? Record them under Teach your own seals.

        KeySealEasy gestureHow

        Pick a seal, press Record, then hold the pose for 3 seconds while moving it slightly. Also record Idle (relaxed hands) to cut false triggers. Samples are kept in this browser.
